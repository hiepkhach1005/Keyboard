## English

`[DEVELOPER NAME]` builds this keyboard with an offline, data-minimizing design.

- We do not collect, retain, sell, or transmit anything you type.
- English and Vietnamese Telex work on device without Allow Full Access.
- The keyboard extension temporarily processes only the text needed for Telex composition. It does not log or persist this content.
- The shared App Group stores preferences only, including language, hold duration, secondary legends, and iPad layout.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, accounts, or tracking SDKs.
- Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal user data for us to sell, share, retain, or delete. Deleting the app removes local data according to iOS behavior.

If features or data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
